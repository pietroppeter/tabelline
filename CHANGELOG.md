- Review mode keeps the comments of older versions: a row of the history brings them to the page's version, where their text is still found, or exports them; **Export everything** replaces *Clear everything*; the export (`nimib-review/5`) says the version a comment was written on, and the skill checks such comments before applying them (#61).
- Every page of the docs has the review mode, turned on by `useTabelline`; a page's version comes from git (the commits that changed its source), shown as *Version N · updated D* beside **✎ Review**, and the export (`nimib-review/4`) names its commit. The post Review mode is one notebook again; its versions 1 to 8 are in git (#58).
- The pages of the docs share their style, fonts and the review script as files in `docs/assets/` (the home page goes from 250 KB to 50 KB); `-d:singleFile` builds a page as one file; `tools/preview.nim` replaces `preview.py` (#55).
- Notebooks `import nimib` whole: nimib removed its old `md` (pietroppeter/nimib#268), so tabelline's `md()` no longer clashes with it; nimib is pinned to that commit until 0.5.0 is tagged. `tools/links.nim` checks links to this repository's own files in the checkout (#50).
- Fix: in a grouped table, a row whose group value is missing was shown twice, before the groups
  and again in the group above it; it is now shown once, before the groups (#53).

## 0.1.0 (10 Nov 2026)

tabelline is great tables for Nim: display tables after R's gt and Python's Great Tables, in
plain Nim, rendered as HTML, text and Markdown, and in nimib notebooks. 0.1 has the basics of gt,
from the data to the footer. The API can still change before 1.0.

- **Data**: `tableData:` (columns written in Nim, with `for`, `if` and computed names), any seq of
  objects or tuples, `parseCsv` and `readCsv` (with `comment = "#"`), and the datasets `planets`,
  `exibble` and `tavolaPitagorica`.
- **The parts of gt**: header, column labels and spanners (also from `a.b` names), stub and stub
  head, row groups, body, footnotes and source notes, caption; `rm*` verbs to take parts away.
- **Formats**: 21, from `fmtNumber` and `fmtPercent` to `fmtNumberSi`, `fmtBytes`, `fmtDuration`
  and `fmtMarkdown`, plus `fmt` with a proc of your own; all five substitutions; text transforms.
- **Columns and rows**: labels, widths, alignment, hiding and moving columns, `rowOrder`,
  `rowGroupOrder`.
- **Styles**: `tabStyle` with `style.*` at every location of Great Tables but the summary ones,
  and `dataColor`, which colours each column on its own.
- **Options**: 56 options through `tabOptions`, 14 `opt*` presets, and `optCssTheme()`, which
  takes every colour and the font from the page's CSS variables, so a table follows its page.
- **Output**: `toHtml` (scoped CSS; `makePage = true` for a whole page), `save`, `echo t` as text,
  `toMarkdown`, and `nbTable` for nimib.

Not in 0.1, and planned: summary rows and merged columns (0.2), formats in every locale (0.3),
nanoplots (0.4), images and other outputs (0.5); see the Roadmap and the Feature map. What
tabelline has that gt does not is listed on the Feature map, under *Not from gt*.

Docs: https://pietroppeter.github.io/tabelline/ (the Guide, the Book, the Milano Cortina 2026
story, the Roadmap, the Feature map, the devlog and the specs).

  Docs: the tokens take the same names (`--n-1` is `--shade`, `--n-3` `--line`, `--n-7` `--muted`,

### The proof of concept (7 Nov 2026)

The first commits, a proof of concept (`fmtBar` and `optQuaderno` were removed before the release).

- The table data (`Value`, `TableData`, `tableData:`, CSV), the table spec (`Tabella`) with the
  six parts of gt (header, column labels and spanners, stub and stub head, row groups, body,
  footer), 11 formatters and `fmtBar`, substitutions, styles at locations, `dataColor`,
  `tabOptions` and presets (`optStylize`, `optQuaderno`, ...), HTML / text / Markdown renderers
  and the nimib block `nbTable`. Datasets: `planets`, `exibble`, `tavolaPitagorica`. (#1)
- Repository setup: OpenSpec specs with runnable examples, the spec page, the explainer, the
  devlog, the quaderno theme and CI for tests, examples and pages. (#1)
- Dependencies with atlas (`atlas.lock`, `nim.cfg`, nimib as the optional `docs` feature) and
  `tools/setup.sh`, which sets up a laptop, CI or a fresh coding-agent sandbox. Needs Nim 2.2
  or later. (#2)
- Docs: an "R or Python?" section and a note on `sink` in the explainer (#2); the devlog post
  *Tabellone: when a table becomes a grid* (#3); backlog ideas from using tabelline in
  diagrammari (#4).
- Docs: a second look for the pages, after Bruno Munari, and the default; the quaderno is a
  switch away in every header, and the choice is remembered. Devlog post *Two looks*. (#5)
