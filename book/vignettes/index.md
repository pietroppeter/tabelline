# gt: articles

Articles of [gt's documentation](https://gt.rstudio.com/articles/), by Richard Iannone and the gt
contributors, MIT licensed: the longer walks through one part of gt, such as summary rows, and the
case studies that build one table from start to finish. Each keeps the text of the original, with
its examples in R (the original), Python (the Great Tables way to make the same table, written for
the book) and Nim; the book's own remarks are in boxes marked “× tabelline”.

What tabelline lacks to do what these articles do is collected in [What the port found](../gaps.html).
