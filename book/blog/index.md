# Great Tables: the blog

Posts of the [Great Tables blog](https://posit-dev.github.io/great-tables/blog/), by Rich Iannone,
Michael Chow and the Great Tables contributors, MIT licensed: the posts that show what a release
brought, now that tabelline can make their tables. Each keeps the text of the original, with its
examples in Python, R (the gt way to make the same table) and Nim; the book's own remarks are in
boxes marked “× tabelline”.

The posts come in the order they were written, from the first: the introduction, then data
colouring, column widths, options and images, and nanoplots.

What tabelline lacks to do what these posts do is collected in [What the port found](../gaps.html).
